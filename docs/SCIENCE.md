## Current limits

- A fictional assemblage around 1,400 m, not a verified co-occurrence record.
  Regional distributions and species-level depth constraints remain future work.
- Sizes are exaggerated. Dumbo and isopod models do not naturally glow. The
  jelly's persistent color is not its episodic blue defensive display.
- Glowing seafloor stalks are fictional scenery, not photosynthetic plants.
  There is no sunlight or surface caustics in this region.
- Visibility is increased for readability. Vent plumes are animated geometry,
  not computational fluid dynamics. No physical current/temperature model yet.
- Capsule engineering, life support, rescue, and mineral gathering are gameplay
  abstractions. This is not a proposed real-world habitat.
- Hadal/abyssal communities, extremophile microbes, chemosynthesis, cold seeps,
  and whale falls are planned, not implemented. Animals cannot be harvested.

## Sources for later development

- [NOAA expedition data](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/data/): imagery,
  observations, and mapping. Inspect credits for third-party exceptions.
- [NOAA 2026 eDNA release](https://oceanexplorer.noaa.gov/news/from-seawater-to-sequences-exploring-noaas-new-deep-sea-environmental-dna-dataset/):
  potential sampling mechanic; detections are not precise live animal locations.
- [OBIS](https://obis.org/): occurrence records with dataset-level licenses and
  citations. Absence of observations does not prove absence of a species.
- [GEBCO](https://www.gebco.net/): large-scale bathymetry reference; local
  navigable detail needs additional data or explicitly procedural generation.
- [NASA Earthdata](https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/): environmental context,
  not direct deep-ocean animal observations.

Identify a product/version, verify license and quality, preserve provenance, and
document processing before importing data. API access does not itself grant
redistribution rights for images or publications.
